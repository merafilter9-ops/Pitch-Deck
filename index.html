The Problem

Trust is broken across the entire supply chain

Three critical user groups suffer from India's fragmented, opacity-ridden product verification ecosystem.

Target User 01

Product Owners & Brands

  • No affordable, accessible lab testing marketplace
  • Cannot gather authentic pre-launch consumer feedback
  • Compliance costs prohibitively high for SMEs
  • Difficult to build trust signals for products

Target User 02

Consumers

  • No way to verify product quality or authenticity
  • Food adulteration affects 1 in 5 packaged goods
  • Zero traceability from factory to shelf
  • Cannot find verified products from nearby stores

Target User 03

Certified Laboratories

  • No central discovery platform — invisible to brands
  • Rely entirely on offline referrals for business
  • No digital tools for order management
  • Massive underutilization of testing capacity

Key Investor Insight
The B2B testing business and B2C quick-commerce business are not separate bets — they are the same flywheel. The testing side funds acquisition; the commerce side drives scale.

Quick Commerce Layer

Zepto for safety-conscious consumers

India's first hyperlocal delivery platform where every product must pass lab verification to be listed. Trust is the entry ticket — not optional.

Retailers onboard & list inventory
Kirana stores and pharmacies list only lab-verified products. One-time product source verification required before listing.
Consumer discovers nearby
App shows verified products within 2–5 km, filterable by category, verified status, and store rating.
Order placed & delivered
Retailer fulfills via Shadowfax/Porter integration (Phase 2) or own staff. Click-and-collect also supported.
Certificate traceable on delivery
Every package has a QR-linked lab certificate. Consumer can verify the test date, issuing lab, and parameters.
Phased delivery approach: Phase 1 uses retailer's own delivery or click-and-collect. Phase 2 integrates third-party logistics API. Own fleet is post-Series A — keeping the model asset-light at launch.

Why Zepto cannot replicate this
Lab verification for every listed product adds cost and complexity that dark-store players have no incentive to absorb. Their margin model runs on volume and speed. Mera Filter's margin model runs on trust — a fundamentally different and defensible competitive position.
